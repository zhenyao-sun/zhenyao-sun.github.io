Probability · Stochastic Analysis

Zhenyao Sun

Assistant Professor at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Beijing Institute of Technology

My research is in probability theory, with a focus on models related to stochastic heat equations.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Faculty of Data and Decision Sciences at the Technion, hosted by Prof. Leonid Mytnik. I received my Ph.D. from Peking University under the supervision of Prof. Yan-Xia Ren, and visited Prof. Renming Song at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I also received my B.S. from Peking University.
